Ocimum tenuiflorum
Essential Oil Nanoformulation

Collaborative formulation research, communicated through a scientific poster and an abstract in the official conference proceedings.

21–23 August 2024NUST · IslamabadPoster T-ICRA-271
The research

From essential oil to a bioactive formulation.

The study explored an essential oil-loaded nanoformulation for potential mosquito-repellent applications, bringing together formulation development, physicochemical characterization and biological activity assessment.

The conference abstract reports antioxidant activity and a concentration-dependent mosquito-repellent response.

My contribution

Co-authored the conference abstract, independently designed the research poster, and presented it as part of a collaborative undergraduate group.
